Walter White

Chemistry Teacher & Iconic Character

Walter White (Heisenberg) in Breaking Bad

About Person

Walter White is the main protagonist of the critically acclaimed television drama series Breaking Bad. Originally a skilled chemistry teacher at a local high school, his life changes drastically after a unexpected medical diagnosis.

Using his deep knowledge of science and chemistry, he transitions into building his own empire under the famous alias "Heisenberg", becoming one of the most memorable characters in TV history.

Key Story Milestones

  • Co-founded: Gray Matter Technologies in his early career.
  • Teaching: Taught high school chemistry at J. P. Wynne High School.
  • Partnership: Teamed up with former student Jesse Pinkman.
  • Legacy: Became a legendary cultural icon in modern pop culture.
